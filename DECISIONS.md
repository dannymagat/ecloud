Approval means **APPROVED DESIGN**, not **VERIFIED DEVICE CAPABILITY**: every device-dependent claim keeps its PHASE2_VALIDATION.md label (REQUIRES DEVICE TEST / UNKNOWN) until the applicable real-device test has actually been executed and passed. A ghost session only arises with a non-conforming NAS that changes CHAP-Challenge between retransmits, and the reaper closes it. Trading a cache-collision risk for a reaped ghost session is a bad trade. **F-006 (MEDIUM, `observeAccessPoint` runs before `identify()`):** NOT changed, intentional. `verified_at` asserts that the AP is online and correctly pointed at this NAS; a RADIUS request arriving at all proves exactly that, and the authentication outcome is irrelevant to it.
