# Architecture Decision Register

Source of truth for material decisions. Statuses: `CONFIRMED` (owner statement or verified evidence), `PROPOSED` (Phase 2 design awaiting owner approval), `OPEN`, `REQUIRES_CLARIFICATION`, `UNVERIFIED`, `REQUIRES_DEVICE_TEST`.

Updated 2026-10-07 after Phase 1 discovery, Phase 2 architecture/protocol validation and the **owner's Phase 2 decision gate (2026-10-07)**.

**Owner approval 2026-10-07:** D-001 through D-020 are APPROVED as *design*, subject to the amendments recorded in D-021 to D-032. Approval means **APPROVED DESIGN**, not **VERIFIED DEVICE CAPABILITY**: every device-dependent claim keeps its PHASE2_VALIDATION.md label (REQUIRES DEVICE TEST / UNKNOWN) until the applicable real-device test has actually been executed and passed. Device behaviour is never inferred from the existence of a configuration or schema path.

Status vocabulary from here on: `APPROVED (design)` = owner-approved design; `REQUIRES_DEVICE_TEST` = device behaviour still to be proven; `CONFIRMED` = owner statement or verified evidence.
